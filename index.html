Hermes Personal Assistant

A private, owner-operated automation tool for Vibe Nest Radio.

What it does

Hermes Personal Assistant connects to the owner’s YouTube account only after the owner grants permission through Google OAuth. It is used to:

  • upload videos and associated metadata that the owner has specifically approved; and
  • retrieve YouTube Analytics reports for the owner’s channel so campaign performance can be recorded and evaluated.

The tool is not offered to the general public and does not provide public registration. It does not ask for or store the owner’s Google password.

Control and transparency

Google authorization can be revoked at any time through Google Account permissions.

For questions about authorization or data handling, use the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
